To ensure compliance and marketability, the project is structured around mandatory certifications:

    \item Udyam Registration (MSME)
    \item GST Certification
    \item Pollution Control Board NOC
    \item Fire Safety NOC
    \item BIS IS 17088:2019 (optional but recommended)
