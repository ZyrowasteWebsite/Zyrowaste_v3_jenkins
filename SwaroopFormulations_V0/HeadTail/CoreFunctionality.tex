\section*{Core Functionalities \& Summary}
This project centers on manufacturing biodegradable bags using Polylactic Acid (PLA) blends. Key operational capabilities include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Compounding of raw PLA materials
    \item Extrusion and film blowing
    \item Printing and sealing
    \item Waste recovery loop
    \item Regulatory tracking system
\end{itemize}
The business model combines eco-compliance, B2B logistics partnerships, and export-readiness to yield a sustainable and scalable production framework.
