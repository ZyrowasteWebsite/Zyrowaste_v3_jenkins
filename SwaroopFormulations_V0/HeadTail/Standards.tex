\section*{National and International Standards Issued}
The manufacturing of PLA-based biodegradable plastic bags aligns with the following standards:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{IS 17088:2019} — Indian Standard for compostable plastics (BIS).
    \item \textbf{ASTM D6400} — USA standard for labeling of plastics designed to be aerobically composted.
    \item \textbf{EN 13432} — European standard for packaging recoverable through composting.
    \item \textbf{ISO 17088:2021} — International standard for compostable plastics.
\end{itemize}
