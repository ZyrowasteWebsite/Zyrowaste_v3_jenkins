\section*{Regulatory Readiness and Compliances}
The unit shall comply with the following requirements:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{GST Registration} – Registered under CGST and SGST.
    \item \textbf{Udyam MSME Registration} – Certified under Government's MSME scheme.
    \item \textbf{PCB Clearance} – NOC from State Pollution Control Board.
    \item \textbf{Fire Safety} – Approval from Fire Department.
    \item \textbf{Trade License} – Issued by Municipal Authority.
\end{enumerate}
