\section{Executive Summary}

This pitch outlines a profitable and environmentally sustainable project focused on biodegradable plastic bag manufacturing using PLA blends. The unit is financially sound, policy-aligned, and has cross-border growth potential.

This proposal outlines a high-potential investment opportunity in the biodegradable plastic bag manufacturing sector. Backed by sustainability trends, government bans on single-use plastics, and favorable economics, this venture is projected to generate:

\begin{itemize}[label=\textcolor{green}{\checkmark}]
    \item Strong EBITDA margins
    \item Positive cash flows
    \item Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) exceeding 3x from Year 1
\end{itemize}

\section{Market Context \& Relevance}

\subsection{Problem Statement}

Traditional plastics are environmentally unsustainable, with decomposition periods extending beyond 500 years. India's 2022 ban on certain single-use plastics underscores the urgency for eco-friendly alternatives.

\subsection{Opportunity}

Biodegradable plastics — particularly those made from Polylactic Acid (PLA), starch blends, and PBAT (Polybutylene Adipate Terephthalate) — offer viable replacements for conventional plastics. Applications include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Grocery bags
    \item Agricultural mulch films
    \item Food packaging
    \item Biomedical components
\end{itemize}
